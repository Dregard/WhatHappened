# Automatic puzzle slicing

## Scope and controls
Read with [.agents/project/DOMAIN_PuzzleAuthoring.md](.agents/project/DOMAIN_PuzzleAuthoring.md) when changing automatic generation, editor contours, piece/background geometry or generated BAKE/Load.
- `Assets/Game/Puzzles/Editor/PuzzleSlicerGenerationData.cs` owns positive requested PieceCount, SizeSpread, normalized ContourComplexity, PreferredMaxAspectRatio (finite >=1), Roundness, CornerSpread, SideSpread, Seed and RandomSeed. The old 256-piece ceiling is removed. Effective count is clamped between four-neighbor visible island count and nonzero-alpha pixel count, with an explanation; requested controls remain unchanged. Fully transparent sources preserve current markup and show an explanation. `Assets/Game/Puzzles/Editor/PuzzleSlicerGenerationView.cs` displays the actual session layer count separately.
- ContourComplexity controls the integer pre-rounding internal side ceiling `4 + floor(146 * complexity * complexity)`, from 4 to 150. The quadratic response retains simple low settings. Higher values refine more cells and enrich shared seams with angular protrusions/recesses. Original alpha edges, holes and silhouette-imposed narrow topology stay fixed and are excluded from the visual side budget. Collinear T subdivisions are one side. Roundness, CornerSpread and SideSpread act afterward; curve samples do not increase this count. SideSpread is user-visible and selects internal sides for single-arc rounding without angular zigzags or alternating waves. Legacy RoundedFraction and 1–8 complexity settings retain their migrations.
- SizeSpread controls visible-pixel targets rather than exact rounded area. Zero seeks equal sizes within topology, raster and rounding constraints. RandomSeed displays a new Seed per request; local System.Random streams preserve Unity random state and reproduce contours and ownership for identical source, settings and seed.

## Partition and rounding
- `Assets/Game/Puzzles/Editor/PuzzleSlicerGenerationController.cs` owns the transaction. Preflight snapshots alpha through `Assets/Game/Puzzles/Editor/PuzzleSlicerPixelMetric.cs` and discovers islands before allocating candidates. Device dimensions and the existing 64 × 1024 × 1024 staged-texel budget still apply. Crops plus working image are budgeted before cutting. Failed validation preserves previous markup; retired/unselected textures and preflight meshes are released.
- `Assets/Game/Puzzles/Editor/PuzzleSlicerPolygonPartitionBuilder.cs` refines connected axis-aligned cells from four-neighbor source islands without losing components or overshooting count. The target is requested count × max(1, floor((MaximumSideCount - 3) * (1 - 0.55 * complexity) / (1 + SizeSpread))), bounded by visible pixels and feasible splits: up to 66 cells per piece at maximum complexity/zero spread. Zero complexity stops at the requested rectangle-based partition; alpha-clipped parts retain the silhouette. Seeded layouts exchange axes, weigh directions by remaining strips, exclude empty cells and sample feasible layouts with inverse-square aspect weights. Connected graph unions then acquire oblique geometry; final float mesh validation remains authoritative.
- `Assets/Game/Puzzles/Editor/PuzzleSlicerPolygonRegionModel.cs` owns connected pixels, bounds and directed unit boundaries. A region whose pixel count equals its bounds area splits directly into two known connected rectangles in row-major order, avoiding floods and bounds rescans; clipped regions retain full component discovery. Graph growth uses `Assets/Game/Puzzles/Editor/PuzzleSlicerPixelFrontier.cs`, existing visible-size targets and four centroid relaxations. Every island gets a distinct seed before farthest-cell sampling. Reversed unit edges cancel; internal straight runs must fit the side ceiling. Alpha-hole interruptions do not multiply sides. Sorted neighbors and canonical frontier ties preserve determinism.
- `Assets/Game/Puzzles/Editor/PuzzleSlicerAngularBoundaryBuilder.cs` moves shared junctions and diversifies their turns. Its bounded 36 coarse-graph trials/pinning/fallback do not scale later seam detail. `Assets/Game/Puzzles/Editor/PuzzleSlicerIrregularSeamBuilder.cs` compacts least-deviation degree-two vertices to max(4, floor(MaximumSideCount * (1 - 0.85 * complexity))), leaving longer spans for expressed protrusions. Source vertices, retraced bridges and multi-owner junctions stay protected.
- `Assets/Game/Puzzles/Editor/PuzzleSlicerSeamEnrichmentController.cs` first reconstructs broad bends, then adds facets on the validated graph. Eight reconstruction groups precede sixteen enrichment groups (eight general, eight requiring expressed depth); rejected groups split up to max(64, nextPowerOfTwo(pieceCount)) so a raster tip cannot flatten unrelated seams as count grows. Both phases use full local clearance independently of coarse displacement fallback. `Assets/Game/Puzzles/Editor/PuzzleSlicerSeamReconstructionBuilder.cs` rebuilds degree-two chains with unequal intervals, a nonperiodic walk and two independent broad bends whose depth scales with complexity squared; closed seams use three anchored arcs. `Assets/Game/Puzzles/Editor/PuzzleSlicerIrregularSeamBuilder.cs` adds one to three vertices per selected edge using both owners' residual budgets, independent clearance, varied spacing, 25–150 degree turns and minimum facet length max(1 pixel, sqrt(piece area) / (4 * sqrt(MaximumSideCount))). Transactions retain exact reversed shared paths and strict graph/raster validation; final float mesh validation stays authoritative. The second enrichment pass requires new depth >= sqrt(mean piece area) * 0.025 * complexity² and both facets >= sqrt(mean piece area) * 0.04 * complexity². Complete enriched contours reject recurring alternating directions.
- `Assets/Game/Puzzles/Editor/PuzzleSlicerAngularPixelValidator.cs` borrows subdivision labels and immutable source membership, checks exclusive complete canonical-scanline ownership and nonempty four-neighbor components, and reuses a flood queue. A failed connectivity sample guides local vertex pinning; final float mesh checks remain mandatory.
- `Assets/Game/Puzzles/Editor/PuzzleSlicerPartitionGenerator.cs` validates final float fill triangles at the source PPU before ranking up to 16 deterministic ForAttempt candidates. Aspect feasibility comes first; impossible proportions retain the smallest actual ratio. Shape ranking uses the worst eligible individual contour, requiring at least 60% non-right internal turns (outside 90±10 degrees) and no run longer than three near-right turns, plus at least 30% seam length inclined ≥10 degrees from both axes. Worst-piece depth quality additionally seeks six visible turns at maximum, with depth >=2.5% and both facets >=4% of sqrt(mean piece area), scaled by complexity squared. Source-imposed corners and turns below 10 or above 170 degrees cannot earn quality; contours with fewer than four eligible turns retain the constrained fallback. Among qualifying candidates, more pre-rounding internal turns of 20–160 degrees win; fallback prefers better worst-piece quality. Search can stop at that quality and mean corners `4 + (MaximumSideCount - 4) * 0.4`. No shape preference discards the only valid partition. `Assets/Game/Puzzles/Editor/PuzzleSlicerAspectRatioUtility.cs` measures the minimum-area oriented rectangle; impossible preferences report actual excess.
- `Assets/Game/Puzzles/Editor/PuzzleSlicerConnectedPartitionBuilder.cs` discovers feasible island/pixel counts; Generate delegates at default PPU100; GenerateAtScale delegates at the imported source PPU to the same polygon path. Its legacy pixel-level GenerateLabels remains for compatibility checks. Current polygon growth reuses the same frontier and `Assets/Game/Puzzles/Editor/PuzzleSlicerVoronoiGenerator.cs` size-target allocator without rasterizing new diagonal stairs.
- `Assets/Game/Puzzles/Editor/PuzzleSlicerRasterBoundaryBuilder.cs` extracts exact shared straight chains, preserving T junctions and source-hole bridges. The polygon builder requests undecorated/unrounded contours. Legacy structural compaction does not define current shapes.
- `Assets/Game/Puzzles/Editor/PuzzleSlicerRoundedBoundaryBuilder.cs` rounds the existing graph in shared junction sectors and side corridors. Complexity no longer scales rounding strength. Repeated bridge traversals by the same owner are not shared seams. `Assets/Game/Puzzles/Editor/PuzzleSlicerSideBoundaryBuilder.cs` samples one selected quadratic side arc, adding no angular bends. Exterior alpha stays fixed; bounded retries reduce rounding clearance on constrained features.
- `Assets/Game/Puzzles/Editor/PuzzleSlicerRoundingPixelPreserver.cs` and `Assets/Game/Puzzles/Editor/PuzzleSlicerPixelSeamController.cs` retain bounded raster size compensation for rounded paths. One-pixel optimization tolerance is not a coverage tolerance: final ownership/connectivity remain strict.
- Legacy Voronoi, recursive intersection, region-merging and connected-ownership helpers remain for compatibility checks outside the current automatic generation path.
